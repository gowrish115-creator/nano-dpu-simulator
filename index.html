🔧 Test Instruments

  
This panel stands in for the weighing scale and milk analyzer that plug into the real DPU. Enter a value, then press Send, exactly like the real instruments feed the app.

  
⚖ Weighing scale
    
    
    
    
Works on: Add Purchase, Add Sale (setting 185 = "1", new sales only), Sample Milk, Data Monitor. Like a real scale, the weight keeps streaming to Add Purchase; after "Remove can" the app shows "--" 5 s later.

  

  
🧪 Milk analyzer
    
    
    
    
    
    
Works on: Add Purchase, Check Rate, Add Sale (setting 188 = "1"), Sample Milk, Cleaning, Data Monitor. Without CLR the app calculates it from FAT/SNF.

  

  
📶 Network
    
    
    
Untick to test the app's offline messages (Sync Now, Online Report, Bonus Bills).
